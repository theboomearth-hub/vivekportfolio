Process

Design run like an engineering discipline

01

Understand

Check the problem is real, write the brief with its kill criteria, then research.

02

Decide

Map findings to psychology principles, then choose the right paradigm: agentic, hybrid, traditional or zero-UI.

03

Design

Flows that include failure paths, and interfaces built on the token system.

04

Ship

Launch against metrics that can't be gamed, and stop when the numbers say so.

Every stage has a gate. Nothing moves forward on instinct alone.
